Pokémon Gym Leaders

Documentation for Kanto and Johto Region Gym Leaders

Kanto Region

Kanto is the first region introduced in the Pokémon series. It is home to 8 Gym Leaders, each specializing in a different Pokémon type. Trainers must defeat all 8 to earn their badges and challenge the Elite Four at Indigo Plateau. Notable locations include Pallet Town, Cerulean City, and Cinnabar Island.

Johto Region

Johto is the region featured in Pokémon Gold and Silver. It is located west of Kanto and shares the same Pokémon League. Johto has 8 Gym Leaders and is known for its rich culture and connection to legendary Pokémon like Ho-Oh and Lugia. Notable locations include Ecruteak City, Goldenrod City, and Blackthorn City.
